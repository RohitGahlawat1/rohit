# Kesariya

Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva (2022)

Description:
"Kesariya" is the lead single from the soundtrack of the 2022 Hindi film
*Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva*, composed by Pritam and sung by Arijit Singh.
The love ballad, picturised on Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt, became one of the
biggest Indian hits of 2022, topping Billboard India and the UK Asian chart.
It won Arijit Singh the Filmfare Award for Best Male Playback Singer and
Amitabh Bhattacharya the award for Best Lyricist.
