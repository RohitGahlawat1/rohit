Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva (2022)

Description:
Kesariya is the chart-topping love anthem from the 2022 Indian film Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva, composed and produced by Pritam with lyrics by Amitabh Bhattacharya. Sung by Arijit Singh, the Hindi soft-rock ballad became the first Indian song to cross 500 million streams on Spotify. Released as a single on 17 July 2022 via Sony Music India.
