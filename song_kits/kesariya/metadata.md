# Kesariya — Publish Kit

Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva (2022)

Description:
"Kesariya" is the lead single from the soundtrack of the 2022 Hindi film *Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva*, composed and produced by Pritam and sung by Arijit Singh. Released by Sony Music India on 17 July 2022, the love ballad topped the Billboard India and UK Asian charts and became the first Indian song to pass 500 million streams on Spotify. Its music video, featuring Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt, was shot in Varanasi and turned the song's hook step into a social-media phenomenon.
