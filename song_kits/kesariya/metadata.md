# Kesariya

Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva (2022)

Description:
"Kesariya" is the lead single from the soundtrack of the 2022 Indian film *Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva*, composed and produced by Pritam and sung by Arijit Singh. Written by Amitabh Bhattacharya, the romantic Hindi track was released by Sony Music India on 17 July 2022 and topped the Billboard India and UK Asian charts. It went on to become the first Indian song to pass 500 million streams on Spotify.
