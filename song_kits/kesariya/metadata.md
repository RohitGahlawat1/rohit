Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva (2022)

Description:
"Kesariya" is the lead single from the 2022 Indian film Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva, composed and produced by Pritam and sung by Arijit Singh. A soft-rock love ballad written by Amitabh Bhattacharya, it topped the Billboard India and UK Asian charts and became the first Indian song to cross 500 million streams on Spotify. The music video, filmed in Varanasi, features Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt.
