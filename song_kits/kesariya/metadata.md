# Kesariya

Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva (2022)

Description:
"Kesariya" is the lead single from the soundtrack of the 2022 Indian film
Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva, composed by Pritam and sung by Arijit Singh.
The romantic love anthem, picturised on Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt, was
released by Sony Music India and became one of the most-streamed Indian songs
of its era.
