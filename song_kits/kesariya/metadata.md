# Kesariya

Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva (2022)

Description:
"Kesariya" is the lead single from the soundtrack of the 2022 Hindi film Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva, composed by Pritam and sung by Arijit Singh. Released on 17 July 2022 by Sony Music India, the love ballad picturised on Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt became one of the biggest Indian songs of the year, topping the Billboard India chart.
