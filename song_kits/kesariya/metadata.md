Title: Kesariya - Arijit Singh | Brahmastra: Part One – Shiva (2022)

Description:
"Kesariya" is the romantic lead single from the soundtrack of the 2022 Hindi film
*Brahmāstra: Part One – Shiva*, composed by Pritam and sung by Arijit Singh. Released by
Sony Music India on 17 July 2022, its music video features Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt.
The track topped the Billboard India and UK Asian charts and became the first Indian song
to pass 500 million streams on Spotify.
